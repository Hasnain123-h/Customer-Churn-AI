CUSTOMER CHURN KNOWLEDGE BASE

Customer churn means when a customer stops using a
company's product or service.

CHURN PREDICTION

The machine learning model predicts whether a customer
is likely to churn.

Prediction:
0 = Customer is likely to Stay
1 = Customer is likely to Churn

TENURE

Tenure represents the number of months a customer has
stayed with the company.

Customers with low tenure may have higher churn risk.

CONTRACT

Contract types include:
- Month-to-month
- One year
- Two year

Month-to-month customers can have higher churn risk
because they have more flexibility to leave.

MONTHLY CHARGES

Monthly Charges represent the amount paid by the customer
each month.

Higher monthly charges can sometimes be associated with
higher churn risk.

TECH SUPPORT

Technical support helps customers solve technical issues.

Poor technical support can reduce customer satisfaction
and may increase churn risk.

RETENTION STRATEGIES

For customers with high churn risk:

- Contact the customer proactively.
- Understand the customer's problem.
- Offer suitable discounts when appropriate.
- Recommend relevant services.
- Improve customer support.
- Offer suitable long-term plans.

IMPORTANT

Churn probability is a machine learning estimate.
It does not guarantee that a customer will actually churn.
